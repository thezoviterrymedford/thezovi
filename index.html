How Zovi works

Two feeds. One big idea.

It starts exactly like the apps you already use. Then it does something none of them can.

1

Your Personal Feed

Just like Instagram or TikTok

Like, save, and import content from TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube — exactly like you already do. Or post your own photos, videos, or quick thoughts. Everything you interact with quietly builds your taste profile. Keep it private and it feeds the algorithm. Go public and let people follow you. Either way, it's the foundation everything is built on.

↓   This is where Zovi stops looking like every other app   ↓
2

Your Group Feed

Nothing else does this

Create a group with your real friends — your crew, your family, your golf buddies, whoever. Every night, Zovi combines everyone's personal feed activity into one shared group feed. Your friend's midnight saves show up next to your sister's recipe finds — automatically, with zero effort from anyone. Nobody sent a link. Nobody forwarded anything. It just appeared. Every morning at 7AM — your crew's drop.

A personal feed alone is just another app. A group feed built from the people you actually trust is something else entirely.

What's in the app

Built different. On purpose.

✓ Core features are completely free — Pro unlocks more
☀️
The Drop
Every morning at 7AM your crew gets a notification: "Your Zovi just dropped." The group feed reveals itself with a staggered animation — like unwrapping something. Same time. Same crew. Every day.
🔒
Private by design
Every group is invite-only. Your group feed is invisible to anyone outside your crew. No strangers, no algorithm, no ads. What happens in your group stays in your group.
🎯
"From Jordan's feed" labels
Every post in your group feed shows exactly which crew member it came from. Never a mystery, never an algorithm. Just your people — and exactly what they were saving.
🏆
Trendsetter Badge
When content your crew saved blows up publicly, Zovi tells you. "Your crew found this 14 days before it went viral." You were early. Your taste called it. Permanent badge on the post.
🔖
The Vault
Saves go to your private Vault — organized into named collections like "Recipes to try" or "Golf tips." Completely private unless you choose to share a collection. Nobody sees your Vault but you.
👻
Ghost Mode
Long press the save button to contribute to your group feed anonymously. Your post appears as "Someone in your crew saved this" — revealed after 24 hours. No social pressure. Just your honest taste.
🎬
Zovi Moment
Once a week, Zovi sends your entire crew a simultaneous alert: "Your crew has 2 minutes." Front and back camera. Raw and real. The result pins to the top of your group feed and lives in your Moments archive forever.
💬
Private crew threads
React to anything in your group feed in a thread only your crew can see. Not public comments — your private group conversation, organized around the content you're all looking at.
🔥
Crew Score & streaks
Your group earns a chemistry score based on how often you independently save the same things. Daily streaks, milestone celebrations, and a crew score that reflects how in sync your tastes actually are.
📱
Import from anywhere
Share a TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube link to Zovi and it silently feeds your taste profile — never posted publicly, never visible in any feed. Just pure signal that makes your group feed smarter.
✍️
Post your own content
Photos, videos, or a quick thought — post original content straight to your personal feed. It shows up in your group feed and, if you're public, in your followers' feeds too.
🧭
Discover & follow
A Discover tab surfaces content matched to your taste profile. Follow public accounts, build your following, and grow beyond your group while keeping your crew feed private.

Questions

The obvious stuff.

Is Zovi really free?
Yes. The free tier is genuinely complete — personal feed, group feed, The Drop, Zovi Moments, private threads, DMs, up to 15 groups. We don't believe in crippling the free experience. Pro adds more of everything, not access to the basics.
Do I have to share anything to use Zovi?
No. That's the whole point. Just use your phone like normal — like and save things you already like. Zovi takes that signal and builds your group feed from it. Nobody had to forward anything. Nobody had to decide what's worth sharing. It just appears.
What does importing from TikTok or Instagram do?
Importing a link never posts it or shares it publicly. It's a taste signal only — it updates your interest profile privately and makes your group feed smarter over time. The imported content never appears in any feed. Nobody can see what you've imported.
Is my group feed private?
Completely. Your group feed is visible only to members of that group. It never appears in Discover. It never surfaces publicly. Group membership is invite-only. What your crew saves is yours alone.
What is the founding member price and what do I get?
$49 one time, lifetime Pro access. After 500 slots this price goes to $4.99/month. Founding members get: lifetime Pro free forever, the Zovi Mark badge, Founding Member profile badge, monthly Group Boost, and day one access before the public launch.
When does Zovi launch?
Beta is launching very soon. Founding members and waitlist signups get access first — before the App Store listing goes live. Join the waitlist or claim a founding member slot to be in the first wave.
What is Ghost Mode?
Long press the save button on any post to contribute to your group feed anonymously. Your save appears as "Someone in your crew saved this" for 24 hours — then your name is revealed. It removes the social pressure of sharing something you love but aren't sure about. Ghost Mode is free (3 per month) and unlimited with Pro.
What is Zovi Moment?
Once a week, Zovi sends your entire crew a surprise notification: "Your crew has 2 minutes." For exactly 2 minutes, everyone can capture a front and back camera shot of what they're doing right now. The grid of captures pins to the top of your group feed for 24 hours, then moves to your permanent Moments archive. It's BeReal — but private to your crew only.
