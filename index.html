How Zovi works

Two feeds. One big idea.

It starts exactly like the apps you already use. Then it does something none of them can.

1

Your Personal Feed

Just like Instagram or TikTok

Like, save, and import content from TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube — exactly like you already do. Or post your own photos, videos, or quick thoughts. Everything you interact with quietly builds your taste profile. Keep it private and it feeds the algorithm. Go public and let people follow you. Either way, it's the foundation everything is built on.

Your Group Feed

Nothing else does this

Create a group with your real friends — your crew, your family, your golf buddies, whoever. Every night, Zovi combines everyone's personal feed activity into one shared group feed. Every morning at 7AM — your crew's drop.

A personal feed alone is just another app. A group feed built from the people you actually trust is something else entirely.

The mechanics

How the group feed actually works.

📱
Step 1 · During the day
You live your feed normally
Like things. Save things. Import a TikTok link. Post a thought. You don't have to do anything different — just use your phone.
⚡
Step 2 · Every night at 2AM
The Builder runs
Zovi looks at what every member of your group saved and liked that day. It ranks the best content from each person and builds a combined feed — automatically, while you sleep.
☀️
Step 3 · Every morning at 7AM
The Drop arrives
You get a notification: "Your Zovi just dropped." Open the app and the group feed reveals itself — post by post, labeled with who found what. "From Jordan's feed." "Someone in your crew." "Your crew found this 12 days early."
💬
Step 4 · React with your crew
Your crew reacts — privately
Every post in the group feed has a private thread only your crew can see. Not public comments — your group's private conversation about what everyone's finding. The living room, not the stage.

Questions

The obvious stuff.

Is Zovi really free?
Yes. The free tier is genuinely complete — personal feed, group feed, The Drop, Zovi Moments, private threads, DMs, up to 15 groups. We don't believe in crippling the free experience. Pro adds more of everything, not access to the basics.
How is Zovi different from Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook Groups?

Great question — Zovi combines the best parts of all of them but solves the problem none of them have cracked.

Instagram shows you what people chose to post publicly. TikTok shows you what an algorithm thinks you want. Facebook Groups require someone to manually share things.

Zovi is different: your group feed is built automatically from what your friends are privately liking and saving — things they never even thought to share. It's like Instagram Reels for your friend group, but way more intimate. Like a Facebook Group, but nobody has to post anything. Like a group chat, but organized around content instead of conversation.

The result is a feed that feels like your friends curated it specifically for you — because they did, without even trying.

Do I have to send things to my friends or notify them when I save something?

Never. That's the entire point of Zovi.

You just use your phone like normal — scroll, like, save, import. Zovi watches those signals silently and builds your group feed from them automatically every night.

Your friends never get a notification when you save something. You never get a notification when they save something. Nobody is performing for each other. Nobody is deciding what's "worth sharing."

At 7AM the next morning the group feed drops and you see what everyone was genuinely into the day before — no curation, no pressure, no effort. Just your crew's real taste.

How does the group feed actually work — what shows up in it?

Every night at 2AM Zovi runs what we call The Builder. It looks at each member of your group and pulls their top activity from that day — posts they liked, things they saved, content they imported, stuff they posted themselves.

Each member contributes up to 3 posts per day on the free tier (more with Pro). The Builder ranks them by a combination of how recently they were saved, how much engagement they got, and how relevant they are to your group's collective taste.

The result drops at 7AM as "The Drop" — a ranked feed of your crew's best finds from yesterday. Every post is labeled "From [Name]'s feed" so you always know who found what. Ghost Mode posts show as "Someone in your crew" for 24 hours before revealing.

The more your crew engages — the smarter and more personalized your group feed gets over time.

Can I be in more than one group?

Yes — up to 15 groups on the free tier, unlimited with Pro. Each group has its own completely separate feed, its own drop time, its own Crew Score, and its own Zovi Moment.

You might have one group for your close friends, one for your golf buddies, one for your family. Each one feels like a distinct space — different color, different emoji, different vibe. Each morning you get separate drops for each group you're in.

The content you save feeds ALL your groups simultaneously — you don't have to think about which group to share something with. Zovi figures out what's relevant for each group based on the collective taste of its members.

What makes the group feed get better over time?

The more your crew uses Zovi, the smarter it gets. Three things make it better:

First, taste profiles. Every like, save, and import updates your interest profile. Over time Zovi knows you love golf and Detroit pizza and indie music — and weights those signals higher in what it surfaces for you.

Second, group convergence. When multiple people in your group independently save the same thing, Zovi promotes it to the top of the next drop. "3 people in Sunday Crew all saved this independently 🔥" — that's the moment you know your crew is really in sync.

Third, streaks. The longer your crew stays active — everyone saving things daily — the higher your group's daily post cap grows. Consistency makes the feed richer.

Do I have to share anything to use Zovi?
No. That's the whole point. Just use your phone like normal — like and save things you already like. Zovi takes that signal and builds your group feed from it. Nobody had to forward anything. Nobody had to decide what's worth sharing. It just appears. Think of it like this: Instagram is a performance stage — you post what you want the world to see. Zovi is a living room — it shows you what your friends are genuinely into when nobody's watching. The intimacy comes from the fact that nobody chose to share anything. It just appeared.
What does importing from TikTok or Instagram do?
Importing a link never posts it or shares it publicly. It's a taste signal only — it updates your interest profile privately and makes your group feed smarter over time. The imported content never appears in any feed. Nobody can see what you've imported.
Is my group feed private?
Completely. Your group feed is visible only to members of that group. It never appears in Discover. It never surfaces publicly. Group membership is invite-only. What your crew saves is yours alone.

$49 one time, lifetime Pro access. After 500 slots this price goes to $4.99/month. Founding members get: lifetime Pro free forever, the Zovi Mark badge, Founding Member profile badge, monthly Group Boost, and day one access before the public launch.
When does Zovi launch?
Beta is launching very soon. Founding members and waitlist signups get access first — before the App Store listing goes live. Join the waitlist or claim a founding member slot to be in the first wave.
What is Ghost Mode?
Long press the save button on any post to contribute to your group feed anonymously. Your save appears as "Someone in your crew saved this" for 24 hours — then your name is revealed. It removes the social pressure of sharing something you love but aren't sure about. Ghost Mode is free (3 per month) and unlimited with Pro.
What is Zovi Moment?
Once a week, Zovi sends your entire crew a surprise notification: "Your crew has 2 minutes." For exactly 2 minutes, everyone can capture a front and back camera shot of what they're doing right now. The grid of captures pins to the top of your group feed for 24 hours, then moves to your permanent Moments archive. It's BeReal — but private to your crew only.

SMS Messaging

Zovi (operated by Circl Technologies LLC) sends one-time SMS verification codes to users who voluntarily provide their phone number during account registration and explicitly request a verification code by tapping "Send verification code" in the app.

How SMS opt-in works:

  1. User downloads the Zovi app
  2. User creates an account
  3. User optionally enters their phone number
  4. User explicitly taps "Send verification code"
  5. User receives a one-time 6-digit code via SMS
  6. No further messages are sent

SMS program details:

  • Message types: One-time verification codes only. No marketing messages.
  • Message frequency: Only when you explicitly request a code. Maximum once per registration attempt.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to immediately unsubscribe.
  • Help: Reply HELP or email hello@thezovi.com
  • Provider: SMS delivered by Twilio. Carrier not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Phone verification is completely optional. You can use Zovi without providing your phone number. We never send marketing messages via SMS. Your phone number is stored only as an encrypted hash — we cannot recover your actual number.
